# Pain — General Home Care Guidance

General, non-diagnostic reference for discussing patient-reported pain.

- Pain is commonly self-reported on a 0-10 scale: 0 means no pain, 10 means
  the worst pain imaginable.
- A pain level of 7 or higher, or any sudden increase from the patient's
  usual baseline, should be logged as an alert for caregiver follow-up.
- New pain in the chest, upper back, or jaw is a distinct category from
  general soreness and should always be flagged as urgent, regardless of
  the numeric score the patient gives.
- Ask about the location and character of the pain (sharp, dull, constant,
  intermittent) in addition to the numeric score — this context matters
  more than the number alone.
- Never suggest a specific medication or dosage for pain relief. Encourage
  the patient to follow their care plan or contact their physician.
