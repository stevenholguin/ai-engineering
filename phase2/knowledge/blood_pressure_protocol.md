# Blood Pressure — General Home Care Guidance

General, non-diagnostic reference for home monitoring of blood pressure.

- Normal resting blood pressure for most adults is roughly 90-120 systolic
  over 60-80 diastolic, though normal ranges vary by individual and by
  physician-set targets.
- Readings above 180 systolic or 120 diastolic should be treated as a
  possible hypertensive emergency — encourage the patient to seek
  immediate care and notify a caregiver right away.
- Readings below 90 systolic or 60 diastolic, especially with dizziness or
  fainting, can indicate hypotension and should also be flagged.
- A single elevated reading is not necessarily an emergency — stress and
  recent activity can temporarily raise blood pressure. Repeated elevated
  readings across several check-ins are more clinically meaningful than one
  isolated reading.
- Always ask whether the patient took their prescribed blood pressure
  medication that day, since missed doses are a common cause of abnormal
  readings.
