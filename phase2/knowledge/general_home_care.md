# General Home Care and Medication Adherence

General, non-diagnostic reference for daily home-monitoring check-ins.

- Ask about medication adherence every day, not just when something seems
  wrong — missed doses are one of the most common causes of preventable
  complications in home-care patients.
- If a patient reports missing a dose, do not tell them whether to take a
  double dose or skip it — this decision depends on the specific
  medication and must come from their physician or pharmacist.
- Dizziness on standing (orthostatic symptoms) is common in older adults
  and can indicate dehydration, blood pressure medication effects, or low
  blood sugar. It should be logged even if the patient considers it minor.
- Encourage brief, consistent daily check-ins over long ones — consistency
  in monitoring is more valuable than the depth of any single check-in.
- The goal of this assistant is monitoring and escalation, never diagnosis
  or treatment decisions.
