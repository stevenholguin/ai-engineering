# Fever — General Home Care Guidance

This document provides general, non-diagnostic guidance for caregivers and
patients on home monitoring. It does not replace professional medical advice.

- A temperature between 37.5°C and 38.0°C is considered a low-grade fever.
  Encourage fluids and rest, and re-check temperature in a few hours.
- A temperature at or above 38.0°C should be logged as an alert and a
  caregiver or nurse should be informed, even if the patient feels otherwise
  well.
- If fever is accompanied by confusion, difficulty breathing, or a rash,
  this should always be treated as urgent regardless of the exact
  temperature reading.
- Fever in patients recovering from recent surgery is more significant than
  in general home-care patients, since it can indicate infection — flag
  these cases for closer follow-up.
- Do not recommend specific medication dosages. Always defer medication
  questions to the patient's prescribing physician or pharmacist.
